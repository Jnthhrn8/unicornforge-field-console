THE PROBLEM

AI is impressive in a chat box—and fragmented everywhere else.

People juggle separate assistants, model subscriptions, automation tools, credentials, files, and devices. Most systems lose context, cannot reliably operate the computer, or claim success without proof.

Advanced local AI remains especially difficult to install, govern, and use from more than one device.

THE PRODUCT

One forge for models, agents, tools, memory, and machines.

01

Private AI command center

Run and switch local models, keep projects organized, attach files, preserve context, and use voice from a desktop or phone.

02

Agentic execution

Break a prompt into tasks, select appropriate models, call tools, show live commands and responses, and verify completion.

03

Personal capability layer

Connect skills, MCP servers, calendars, Obsidian, development tools, browser control, and user-managed credentials.

04

Cooperative compute

Pair trusted Forge computers and distribute suitable work across available machines while preserving owner control.

05

Continuous personalization

Curate consented interactions into reviewable training candidates and build a model harness around the individual.

06

Observable autonomy

Expose agent activity, failures, reasoning artifacts, health, resource use, approvals, and human takeover instead of hiding them.

WHY THIS CAN MATTER

The defensibility is the system around the model.

  1. Model independenceForge can evolve with open and commercial models instead of depending on one provider.
  2. Personal operating contextLong-lived user preferences, workflows, projects, and approved data create compounding utility.
  3. Execution and verificationTool routing, observable activity, and evidence-based completion address the gap between answers and outcomes.
  4. Trusted device meshMultiple user-owned computers can become a coordinated private compute surface.

BUSINESS MODEL HYPOTHESES

Monetize the trusted workspace, not the user’s private data.

  • Paid desktop license or subscription for the managed Forge experience
  • Pro tier for multi-computer orchestration, advanced agents, and managed updates
  • Team deployments, support, and workflow configuration
  • Optional model, training, or compute services with explicit user choice

These are hypotheses to validate during alpha; pricing and unit economics have not been finalized.
