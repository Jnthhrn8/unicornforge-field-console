BEFORE YOU BEGIN

The unabridged field-test guide

Plain language comes first. Important legal and data details are not hidden behind tiny links.

01

What this is

Unfinished pre-alpha software. Features, models, tools, availability, and saved data may change or fail. It is not a professional, emergency, medical, legal, financial, or safety-critical service.

02

What you receive

One isolated tester account with one chat, personal memory, up to four named personalities, live task activity, and access to whichever Forge computer is online.

03

What we collect

Account details, onboarding answers, chats, responses, feedback, timestamps, browser and approximate hardware information, network address, tool activity and output, errors, access changes, and structured debugging logs. Passwords and authentication secrets are excluded from debugging logs.

04

Why we collect it

To operate and personalize the test, diagnose failures, evaluate model and tool behavior, improve safety and usability, and create de-identified training/evaluation material.

05

Human review

The Unicorn can review chats and activity while they are happening, pause model replies, enter a conversation, and reply directly. Human replies are visibly labeled “This message is from The Unicorn.” Do not submit passwords, payment details, government IDs, medical records, confidential work, or other sensitive information.

06

Retention and exports

At completion you receive the original record and a redacted portable copy. Original account data is then deleted. A de-identified copy may be retained for training, testing, and evaluation.

07

Your choices

Participation and public recognition are voluntary. You may stop or request export and deletion. A redacted record already incorporated into a completed aggregate evaluation or model artifact may not be retractable.

08

Security

Access requires tester credentials through the approved HTTPS tester website or private Tailscale connection. Administrative actions require separate owner authentication. No system is perfectly secure.

09

Adaptive conversation style

Forge analyzes recent conversation wording locally for temporary interaction signals such as urgency, frustration, playfulness, seriousness, and desired detail. It uses those signals to adjust delivery—not to diagnose you or create a permanent psychological profile. A general style-mode label may appear in debugging and evaluation records.
